# Domain: Research

## Overview

The research domain is intended to support strategy experimentation, evaluation, and analysis workflows.

It will provide tools for:

- strategy iteration and testing
- experiment tracking
- parameter tuning and comparisons
- analysis of performance metrics

---

## Current Status

This domain is not yet implemented.

There are no research workflows, experiment tracking systems, or analysis tools integrated into the platform.

---

## Planned Responsibilities

- Manage strategy experiments and configurations
- Track experiment results and metadata
- Support parameter sweeps and comparisons
- Integrate with backtesting outputs
- Provide analysis and reporting capabilities

---

## Notes

The research domain will build on top of the backtesting system and storage layer, using RunManifest and dataset versioning to ensure reproducibility.
